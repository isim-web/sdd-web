Hello, I am Sonia

A quiet force for
transformative leadership.

For over 16 years, I have worked at the intersection of image, behaviour, and communication. I believe that true executive presence is not about performing a role, but about aligning how you look, sound, and act with the leader you are becoming.

Dubbed a "Fairy Godmother of Transformation" by the media, my work is rooted in a simple truth: the higher the position, the less the title can do the work for you. I help leaders and teams build the quiet authority required to command any room.
